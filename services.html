A dosha is your unique biological constitution — the specific blend of elements (air, fire, water, earth, space) that governs how your mind and body function. Ayurveda identifies three primary doshas: Vata (air + space), Pitta (fire + water), and Kapha (earth + water). Understanding your dominant dosha is the key to knowing what nourishes you — and what throws you off balance.
