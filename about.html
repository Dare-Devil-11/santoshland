The Founder's Story

She grew up with ancient wisdom.
Western medicine left her unheard.

Priya Santosh was born into an Indian household where the medicine cabinet held tulsi, ashwagandha, and triphala before it held anything from a pharmacy. Her grandmother made oil infusions from scratch. Her mother's kitchen was her first pharmacy. Ayurveda wasn't a lifestyle trend — it was simply how her family lived.

In her twenties, Priya entered the Western medical world — first as a patient, then as a student. She was bright, driven, and deeply curious. But something was missing. The system was excellent at labeling what was wrong. It was less interested in why. Patients arrived with chronic fatigue, skin flare-ups, hormonal imbalances, and persistent anxiety — and left with a prescription that addressed the surface, not the source.

After years of watching people cycle through treatments without resolution, Priya returned to the tradition she had grown up with. She completed her Ayurvedic Practitioner certification through NAMA — the National Ayurvedic Medical Association — and trained under master herbalists in both India and the United States. She added clinical herbalism and Panchakarma specialization to her practice, weaving together the precision of modern wellness science with the depth of traditional Ayurvedic protocol.

Santoshland — "the land of contentment" in Sanskrit — was born from that reunion. It is not a supplement shop, a wellness app, or a generic meal plan. It is a genuine, personalized healing practice built for people who have tried everything that treats the symptom and are now ready to address the root.

Today, Priya and the Santoshland team have guided more than 500 clients through their healing journeys. Some came with autoimmune flares. Others with lifelong digestive chaos, chronic stress, or hormonal fog. Every single one received a protocol that was built entirely around them — their dosha, their history, their season of life. No two clients have ever received the same plan. That is not a selling point. It is the point.

How We Work

Our approach in five steps.

Structured. Personal. No shortcuts. This is how every client relationship unfolds — from first conversation to lasting results.

Step One

Listen Deeply

Your first consultation is unhurried. We want the full picture — not just your primary complaint. Sleep, digestion, stress patterns, relationships, seasons that affect you most. Everything is relevant to your dosha and your protocol.

Step Two

Assess Holistically

We evaluate your dosha constitution, current imbalances (vikruti), lifestyle, diet, and seasonal context. The assessment is layered — physical, mental, and energetic — because that is how Ayurveda sees the whole person.

Step Three

Personalize Fully

Your protocol is built from scratch. Herbal formulations, dietary adjustments, daily routine (dinacharya), breathwork, and seasonal modifications. Nothing is copied from a template. Your plan exists for no one else.

Step Four

Support Consistently

Healing is not linear. Weekly or bi-weekly check-ins let us track your response, adjust your protocol as the season or your body shifts, and make sure you feel genuinely supported — not just sold a product and left alone.

Step Five

Celebrate Your Healing

Lasting results. Not a dependency on supplements or ongoing consults forever — but the knowledge and tools to maintain your balance independently. That is the Ayurvedic goal: self-sufficiency through self-knowledge.
